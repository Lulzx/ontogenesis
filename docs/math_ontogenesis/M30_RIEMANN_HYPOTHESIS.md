# M30 — Riemann Hypothesis

## Required outcome

M30 permits only two outcomes: a formally checkable proof of RH, or a formally
checkable reduction to a substantially smaller explicit unresolved statement.
The checker and ontology must be trusted independently of the search.

## Status: not reached

The implemented ladder currently reaches M10 and documents the M11 auxiliary-
object/existential-proof boundary. No claim about proving RH, reducing RH, discovering a novel
equivalence, or constructing an RH-making object is made. Finite verification
of zeros cannot satisfy M30.

## Why this file exists

It records the exact terminal criterion and prevents directory completeness
from being confused with theorem completion. Advancing this status requires
all earlier dependencies, an auditable trusted kernel, complete proof terms,
explicit assumptions, reproducible resource accounting, and independent expert
verification. Until then the correct scientific result is “not reached.”
