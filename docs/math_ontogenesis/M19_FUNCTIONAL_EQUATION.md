# M19 — Invent a Functional Equation

## Required discovery

From observations in different parts of a toy zeta object's domain, the system
must discover both a reflection center and an auxiliary transformation relating
the regions, such as `F(s)=factor(s)F(c-s)`.

## Status: not attempted

M18's prerequisite analytic object does not exist in the implemented track.
There is also no complex symbolic-function grammar, domain-aware equality
checker, or continuation mechanism. Finite numerical symmetry fitting would be
insufficient.

## Completion criteria

Search must hide the transformation and center, compare candidate symmetries by
total theory compression, verify the identity symbolically or within a trusted
finite toy algebra, and include asymmetric controls. The discovered relation
must reduce extrapolation/reasoning cost outside the fitting region.
