# M17 — Invent an Euler Product in a Finite Toy World

## Required discovery

A globally defined sum over a finite arithmetic universe must be transformed
into a product over irreducible elements. Prime factorization cannot be given
as the desired representation.

## Status: not attempted

M7 discovers a common-divisor invariant, but the repository has no generic
finite arithmetic universe, irreducible-object discovery, multiplicative
function language, or product/sum equivalence prover. Earlier proof stages are
also unresolved.

## Valid future result

The system must infer primitive factors from multiplication behavior, construct
local factors, verify that expanding their product accounts for every global
object exactly once, and demonstrate coefficient/special-value savings on
held-out finite universes. A supplied list of primes or prewritten Euler factor
template would not count as invention.
