# M14 — Invent Symmetry as a Representation

## Required discovery

M14 asks the system to invent input transformations that preserve or negate
observed outputs, then reuse that abstraction across functions, geometry,
polynomials, matrices, and finite groups. “Even,” “odd,” and “symmetry” are not
supplied.

## Status: attempted — exact L2 result, L3 criterion failed

The frozen experiment in `NEXT_BOUNDARY.md` constructed `input * -1` from
generic scalar arithmetic, independently checked its two-sided inverse, and
found identity and sign-reversing output-response programs. Aggregate actual
downstream proposal checks fell 123→22 across polynomial, geometry, matrix,
and bounded cyclic tasks. Two frozen controls nevertheless showed negative
transfer (8→9 and 7→9), so the pre-registered every-family requirement failed.
The result is retained as L2 and is not reported as L3 acquisition.

## M14c repair: L3 reached

The failed unconditional policy remains part of the record. A separately
pre-registered experiment retained the complete action–response schema and
routed it using identical raw intervention probes in both conditions. On nine
new frozen tasks, six compatible families accelerated and three incompatible
controls were unchanged. Exact checker calls fell 328→176; 54 common probe
evaluations per condition are separately labeled. With zero false-positive
routes and zero negative-transfer tasks, M14c reaches
`L3_transferred_ontology_with_measured_utility`.

This does not claim invention of arbitrary group actions: pointwise scalar
lifting, intervention probing, and transformation stability as an objective
were supplied.

M2 and M7 discovered invariants, and M9 found special directions, but none
searches transformations as first-class objects across heterogeneous domains.
The ordered ladder is already stopped at M10, so no M14-specific architecture
has been added.

## Evidence required

Completion requires a transformation grammar, composition and inverse tests,
invariant/equivariant predicate discovery, held-out cross-domain transfer, and
cost reduction versus storing pairwise coincidences. Degenerate constant
functions must be a control. Merely recognizing `f(x)=f(-x)` from supplied
pairs would not establish the reusable representation demanded here.
