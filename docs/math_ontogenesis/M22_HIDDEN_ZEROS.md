# M22 — Infer Hidden Zeros From Arithmetic

## Required discovery

Zeros are withheld. From counting functions, errors, or arithmetic
oscillations, the system must invent latent oscillators, complex exponents, and
the corresponding hidden spectral locations.

## Status: not attempted

There is no M18–M21 toy analytic world from which suitable arithmetic
observables can be generated. M9's latent-direction search is discrete linear
dynamics and cannot invert an explicit-formula-like signal.

## Completion evidence

A future protocol needs blinded synthetic arithmetic observables, model-order
selection, complex-frequency inference, reconstruction of held-out
oscillations, and comparison to non-spectral baselines. Identifiability limits
and aliasing must be reported. Feeding zeros into feature construction or
fitting frequencies only after inspecting them would invalidate the claim.
