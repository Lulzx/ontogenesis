# M15 — Invent the Fourier Representation

## Required discovery

Given complicated periodic signals, M15 must invent reusable oscillatory
coordinates that reduce description, prediction, and composition costs. Basic
trigonometric/function primitives may be supplied, but Fourier decomposition
may not.

## Status: attempted — exact L2 result, L3/meta-transfer gate failed

The pre-registered experiment generated coordinates from generic bounded
second-order recurrences, not a named frequency or trigonometric dictionary.
It found 32 atoms and exact closed-shift families. Five frozen phase, amplitude,
mixture, and cross-family composition tasks reconstruct and predict exactly;
description falls from 72 stored samples to 36 integers. Exact false controls
are rejected and noisy data has explicit squared error 2.

The candidate set was identical with and without the retained M9 idea. Trying
closed-dynamics families first helped two tasks but harmed three: actual checks
rose from 1,668,649 unguided to 16,484,585 guided. Thus the representation result
is L2, not acquired L3 utility, and M16 remains blocked on a safer endogenous
applicability mechanism. No post-output task or threshold tuning was performed.

M9's simple-dynamics concept is therefore relevant but not safely transferable
as an unconditional priority. The result remains finite, exact, integer-valued,
and recurrence-bounded; it does not establish continuous Fourier analysis,
orthogonality, projection, or general frequency estimation.

## M15b repair: conditional routing reaches L3

The failed unconditional policy remains part of the record. A separately
pre-registered repair retained M15's closed-shift coordinate-pair schema and
routed it with six common raw samples per task: signals consistent with a
single atom are declined, signals consistent with an exact closed-shift pair
are routed to the guided ordering, and everything else is declined. On sixteen
new frozen tasks, nine closed-pair tasks accelerated (134,049→12,423 checks),
two compatible single-atom/non-closed tasks and all five controls kept their
baseline counts, and the aggregate fell 223,737→102,111. Probe evaluations are
96 per condition and separately labeled; false-positive routes and
negative-transfer tasks are both zero; impulse, ramp, corruption, and noise
controls are rejected as exact laws. M15b reaches
`L3_transferred_ontology_with_measured_utility` while recurrence generation,
the closed-shift objective, and the probe mechanism remain supplied.

## Future evidence

A valid experiment needs answer-blind basis search, diverse train/held-out
signals, comparison against time-domain storage and generic function fitting,
phase/amplitude recovery, composition tests, noise and non-periodic controls,
and explicit approximation error. Hard-coding a sine/cosine dictionary labeled
“Fourier” would weaken the intended discovery claim.
