# M21 — Invent the Critical Symmetry Locus

## Required discovery

Given zeros of the completed toy object, M21 must infer the simplest geometric
locus jointly explaining zero positions, functional reflection, conjugation,
and other relations. A line or axis concept is not supplied.

## Status: not attempted

The M20 completed object and its zeros do not exist in the implemented track.
The current geometry search in M2 handles real point invariants only and cannot
represent complex conjugation or compare competing loci by multi-phenomenon
compression.

## Future controls

The locus grammar must include alternatives such as circles, paired curves,
unions, and reflection orbits; otherwise a line is effectively hard-coded.
Held-out zeros and perturbed-zero controls are necessary. Preference must be
driven by joint compression, not merely zero residual on a finite point set.
