# M29 — Search for an RH-Making Object

## Required discovery

Invent an object `X` and property `P(X)` such that `P(X)->RH`, while `X`
naturally explains independent zeta and prime phenomena. Possible search
spaces include operators, dynamics, geometry, measures, categories, spectra,
and combinatorial structures.

## Status: M29a invalidated; M29b surrogate reached; M29 unreached

M26–M28 provide a completed object, finite-data locus conjecture, and exact
predicate reformulation, but not RH. M29a therefore freezes a narrower exact
test over coefficient-derived even quartics. It measures whether the M25
forcing-object pattern transfers without zero-coordinate provenance.

## Scientific safeguards

Future work must penalize object complexity, prohibit encoding zero locations
directly, separate construction evidence from evaluation evidence, require
explanations of multiple independent observables, and expose the residual proof
obligation exactly. A suggestive spectral analogy without a checked implication
does not count.

Even a passing surrogate does not reach this milestone. Its even-quartic
family and quadratic sign checker are D20 debt, and it explains no independent
prime phenomenon. The record must keep `real_zeta_object=false`,
`rh_proved=false`, and `m29_reached=false`.
