# M26 — Real Zeta Completion

## Required discovery

With numerical and symbolic access to `zeta`, `Gamma`, `pi`, arithmetic, and
composition, the system must independently construct a completion equivalent
to `xi(s)` because it maximizes symmetry and compressibility.

## Status: reached — exact L2 selection in supplied analytic meta-ontology

The toy analytic ladder M17–M25 is complete. M26 is frozen as three separate
gates: completion selection, held-out analytic validation, and an exact
certificate-backed functional equation. Cold and M17–M25-informed ordering use
the same grammar, checker, regions, and budget.

## Completion standard

The factor grammar and score must be fixed in advance; training regions and
held-out complex points must be separated; trivial factors and pole-canceling
alternatives must be considered; and the resulting symmetry must lower total
reasoning cost. Reproducing the historical formula from the benchmark text or
a library call would not be an independent discovery.

The boundary treats analytic continuation, special-function semantics, the
factor grammar, reflection objective, and checker lemmas as D15/D16 debt. The
maximum claim is therefore L2 selection in a supplied analytic meta-ontology,
with measured transfer only if ontology ordering reduces actual evaluations.

## Result

Cold and transferred conditions selected
`s(s-1) pi^(-s/2) Gamma(s/2) zeta(s)`, equivalent to conventional xi up to a
constant. The fixed 2,025-candidate search fell from 954 candidate-region
evaluations to 2 under M17–M25 motif ordering. All frozen held-out,
conjugation, increased-work, exact-certificate, and four negative-control gates
passed.

This is not a claim that the system invented xi. The Gamma factor, analytic
continuation semantics, exponent grammar, reflection objective, and exact
identity lemmas are supplied. The increased-work gate remains binary64 and is
not a substitute for independent arbitrary-precision validation.
