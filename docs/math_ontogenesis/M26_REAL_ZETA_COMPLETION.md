# M26 — Real Zeta Completion

## Required discovery

With numerical and symbolic access to `zeta`, `Gamma`, `pi`, arithmetic, and
composition, the system must independently construct a completion equivalent
to `xi(s)` because it maximizes symmetry and compressibility.

## Status: not attempted

The toy analytic ladder M17–M25 is unfinished, so moving directly to real zeta
would violate milestone order. The repository also lacks validated complex
special-function evaluation, symbolic identity checking, analytic continuation,
and singularity-aware search.

## Completion standard

The factor grammar and score must be fixed in advance; training regions and
held-out complex points must be separated; trivial factors and pole-canceling
alternatives must be considered; and the resulting symmetry must lower total
reasoning cost. Reproducing the historical formula from the benchmark text or
a library call would not be an independent discovery.
